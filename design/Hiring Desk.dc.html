How scoring works

Gemini scores each criterion 1–5 from evidence in the anonymised CV. This page does the maths: weighted score = Σ weight × score ÷ 5, out of 100. 75+ advance · 60–74 interview and probe the lowest criterion · under 60 pass. Scores within 2 points of a line are borderline. Gate (not scored): Mumbai-based or willing to relocate — if a CV doesn’t say, the invite asks.
