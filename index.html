    How scoring works

    Gemini scores each criterion 1–5 from evidence in the anonymised CV. This page does the maths: Σ weight × score ÷ 5, out of 100.

    75+ advance · 60–74 interview, probe the lowest criterion · under 60 pass. Within 2 points of a line = borderline.

    Gate (not scored): Mumbai-based or willing to relocate. If a CV doesn’t say, the invite asks.
